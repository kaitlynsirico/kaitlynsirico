Data Analyst
------------

I am a Data Analyst with 4 years of experience running my own business in Charlotte. In 2024, I earned my Data Analytics Certification from Springboard, where I focused on statistical and financial analysis, predictive modeling, regression analysis, and data wrangling.
Currently, I’m working with a financial literacy non-profit, where I provide analytics to track their success rates and support reporting to donors and grants. This year, I analyzed data across various classes, helping the organization identify trends in attendance and engagement. By streamlining reporting processes, I reduced leadership's time spent on compiling reports from hours to just 10 minutes, freeing them to focus on strategic initiatives and program development. Previously, I developed a predictive model that accurately forecasted a 34% employee attrition rate at a 4,000-person company, allowing leadership to take proactive measures.
I’m proficient in using tools like Power BI, Tableau, Excel, SQL, and Python to deliver actionable insights. My 4 years of experience as a business owner have given me a strong foundation in understanding business needs, collaborating with stakeholders, and using data to solve complex problems and improve organizational outcomes.
